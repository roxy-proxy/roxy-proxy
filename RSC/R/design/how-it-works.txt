a:T45b,One exchange through roxy, top to bottom. The client's request enters roxy and passes through four stages: decrypt and parse (CONNECT, TLS, strict parse into the canonical request, and the lease check), the addon stack (enforce layers in order, with observers beside them on a copy), the rules (lease checked again; deny wins; effects: secrets, headers, redirect, sign) and connect (DNS, address floor, upstream TLS), then reaches the upstream. The response returns up the same stages: re-framed by the connector, re-checked by the rules, seen by the addons, and canonically re-serialised to the client. Each stage has an exit branching to the right: a parse failure is refused with parse_error, an expired policy denies with _expired at either lease check, an addon can answer the client directly or deny with layer:name, the rules deny with the rule id, and the address floor denies with _address_policy. A dotted line between the rules and connect marks where capture records what the rules allow.
